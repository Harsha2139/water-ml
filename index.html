👋 Welcome — upload your data to begin

This site has no built-in data. Once you upload a file it trains 4 ML models and unlocks: prediction, what-if analysis, trends, rainfall impact, district comparison, risk map, depletion alerts, Normal/Warning/Critical classification, model comparison, feature importance, forecasting, historical comparison, recommendations and prediction history.

Columns needed: depth (water level, m) + year and month (or one date column). Optional: district, state, rainfall, temperature, latitude & longitude (for an accurate map). Header names are detected automatically (e.g. "Rainfall (mm)", "Water Level", "Station"). Minimum 3 years and 60 rows.

Auto-set from your data (40th and 75th percentile of depth). Change them to your own standards.
Status thresholds (depth in m): Warning from Critical above

PDF reading needs internet (pdf.js loads from cdnjs) and works only for text-based PDFs with tables, not scans. Always check the preview. 🔒 Your data never leaves this browser.
